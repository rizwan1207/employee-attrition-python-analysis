# employee-attrition-& Retention Analysis (Python)
##Project Overview
An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analsyis (EDA) project in **python** investigating the key factors driving employee turnover (attrition). the goal is to provide actionable HR insights to improve employee retention and reduce talent churn.
---
##Key Findings & Business Insights
**Overtime & Work-life Balance:** Employees Frequently working overtime showed significantly higher attrition rates compared to those with standard work hours.
**Monthly Income & Salary Hikes:** Attrition was heavily concentrated in lower salary slabs, highlighting compensation as a primary retention driver.
**Tenure & Role Stagnation:** Employees within their first 1-2 years in a role or company exhibited the highest attrition risk.
**Departmental Breakdown:** Sales and R&D Departments accounted for the highest turnover volume.
